💎 About InfoJob Corporate Intelligence
1,052 Verified Companies Official Brønnøysund Data High B2B Commercial Value Instant Due Diligence Zero Hallucinations
InfoJob is an autonomous Norwegian corporate intelligence platform engineered to provide instant, verified business profiles and executive intelligence for over 1,000+ enterprises across Norway. By integrating official statutory registries directly with verified live web intelligence, InfoJob eliminates hours of manual research for procurement teams, financial analysts, B2B sales professionals, and compliance officers.
Authoritative Entity Verification

Directly connected to Norway's official national registers (Brønnøysundregistrene & Enhetsregisteret) under NLOD 2.0 open government data:

  • Statutory Identity: Verifies registered legal names, 9-digit enterprise codes, founding dates, and official municipality addresses.
  • Industry Classification: Precise NACE industry sector tagging to pinpoint business operations and competitive verticals.
  • Active Operating Status: Real-time confirmation of active trading versus liquidation, insolvency, or bankruptcy flags.
Executive Leadership & Governance

Unlocks complete corporate governance structures to eliminate counterparty risk and accelerate B2B transactions:

  • Board & Management Oversight: Identifies Board Chairs (Styreleder), Chief Executives (Daglig Leder), and registered signatories.
  • Authorized Representation: Maps signing authorities and certified corporate auditors for legally binding agreements.
  • Workforce Scale: Accurate registered employee headcount tracking corporate growth and enterprise footprint.
Financial Health & Risk Telemetry

Surfaces essential corporate fiscal performance and stability metrics for confident commercial decision-making:

  • Turnover & Operating Margin: Submitted revenue figures, operating results (EBIT), and profitability trends.
  • Capital & Equity Health: Audited equity structures, assets, and liability obligations from annual accounting submissions.
  • Commercial Risk Rating: Transparent solvency signals that help credit managers and vendors evaluate partnership viability.
Live Web Footprint & Verification

Bridges static government filings with dynamic real-world public digital intelligence:

  • Domain Resolution: Cross-checks official company websites and corporate digital portals to prevent fraudulent impersonation.
  • Executive Mentions & News: Live indexing of corporate press releases, media milestones, and public executive announcements.
  • Sub-Second In-Memory Access: Instant search response across 1,000+ pre-indexed enterprises without external latency.

Autonomous Norwegian Corporate Intelligence Studio designed to query, disambiguate, verify, and maintain 1,000+ Norwegian enterprise dossiers with 100% Modulo-11 mathematical accuracy and official Brønnøysund provenance.
